Your figures, where your people are

Customers want to follow their orders without calling you. Partners want their results. Your team wants the numbers on a screen, not in a spreadsheet. Website Dashboard Pro lets you design a dashboard once and show it on a website page, in the customer portal, inside any page of your site and in the Odoo back end. Each dashboard decides who may open it and whose data it shows, and every figure follows Odoo's access rules. The dashboard engine and the charts are included: no other app to buy.

Getting started

Install Website Dashboard Pro from the Apps menu. Open Dashboards, Configuration, Create from Template, pick an app and tick Publish on the Website. In the dashboard's Website tab choose who may see it and whose data it shows.

Questions

Do I need another dashboard app? No, the engine and the charts are included.

Can each customer see only their own data? Yes, with Viewer's data the figures follow their access rights.

Can visitors who are not signed in see a dashboard? Yes, if you set its visibility to Everyone.

Does it work on phones? Yes, the items are stacked on small screens.

Does it use an external service? No, everything runs in your Odoo.
